## Recommendations for future students
1. Start working early, order components and PCBs early. This would be very helpful for your team to troubleshoot any potential issues you face while soldering your board and programming it.

2. Try 3D printing or machining your design/frame to include your PCBs and electronics. This would be very professional and helpful to showcase your concept design more clearly.

3. Ensure that you spend enough time outside of this class. Make sure to attend meetings and come to every class on time. Participation and communication in team meetings are highly important and valuable, helping you stay on track to complete tasks and finish the subsystem.
